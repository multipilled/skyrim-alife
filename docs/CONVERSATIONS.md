# Conversations: how squads talk to you

Everything between you and a squad is ordinary Skyrim dialogue (the dialogue menu, subtitles, the NPC's own lines). There are no
message boxes and no hotkeys. A squad speaks first only when it has a reason; otherwise you start the conversation by
activating one of them, like anyone else.

## Three kinds of conversation

| Mode | Who | What they say | Your choices |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Toll** | bandits that think they hold the advantage | call a toll from a distance, walk up, repeat it | *Pay the toll* (needs the gold) / *I don't have that much* / *I'm not paying* / *Come and take it, then* |
| **Challenge** | soldiers, Thalmor, Dawnguard, Vigilants (armed stranger they distrust); Forsworn, necromancers, vampires, the Silver Hand (you are on their ground) | demand your business | *I mean no harm* / *I don't answer to you* |
| **Greeting** | anyone who regards you neutrally or better | an ordinary hello | *Let me see what you have for sale* (opens the barter menu on their real pack) / *What's the word on the road?* (a line, then a real recent event) |

Later milestones added more modes on the same globals (`include/ALife/Talk.h` `Talk::Mode`): job topics in the greeting
(docs/TASKS.md), 4 companion (docs/COMPANIONS.md), 5 wounded and 6 yielded (docs/WOUNDED.md).

Consequences: paying a toll lets you go (they remember). Pleading poverty is believed if you are poor. Refusing, fighting
or defying turns the squad on you, but never *during* the conversation: it takes effect once the menu has closed. Peace buys a
few hours of being left alone. Trade and news raise a squad's memory of you a little.

If you walk away without choosing, the squad's patience runs out in a few seconds; if you draw steel at the table it
ends badly. If a squad cannot reach you (a hillside in the way), it catches up out of sight, or loses your trail after 90 seconds
without anyone having been refused.

## How it is built (for modders)

- `ALifeTalk.esp` is generated by `tools/build_talk_esp.py`. One Hello topic (category Misc, subtype 79) holds the opening
  lines, one top-level topic per choice holds the answers. Every INFO is conditioned on `GetGlobalValue(ALifeTalkMode)`,
  `GetInFaction(ALife faction)` and, for money, `GetItemCount(gold)` against `ALifeTollGold`.
- **The records belong to the vanilla `DialogueGeneric` quest (Skyrim.esm 0x13EB3).** A quest of our own is never consulted by
  the engine for a squad member: the Hello and the topics were skipped even with every condition removed, even with the actor in
  a quest alias, even after restarting the quest. As Dawnguard and Dragonborn do, add to the generic quest.
- The plugin sets `ALifeTalkMode` / `ALifeTollGold` for the squad the player is looking at (or the one that is walking up) a few
  frames before the engine picks the greeting, and learns the choice from `TESTopicInfoEvent` (`config/talk.ltx` maps each response
  FormID to an action), so no Papyrus fragment is needed.
- A squad opens the conversation by *activating its leader as the player* (`ActivateRef`): `Actor::SetDialogueWithPlayer` only
  sets a flag that a ForceGreet package acts on.
- A choice's consequence is stored and applied by the squad's next tick, after the menu has closed. Starting a fight (or calming a
  squad) from inside the dialogue event hung the game.
- Trade: after the conversation, the console's `ShowBarterMenu` runs on the speaker. It works on any actor and trades over its
  inventory and gold, which is why a squad's pack is real (`Economy`) and why trade is not limited to merchants.
- **Find vanilla topics by their 4-character subtype code (SNAM), not by a library's subtype names.** Mutagen 0.51.5 mislabels
  many combat and crime topics: Skyrim.esm 013EE4 is named "PowerAttack" but is DETH (death), 03E248 is named "Hit" but is
  BLOC (block). Lines wired by the name land on the wrong topic and the real one stays silent. `DialogueGeneric` also holds a
  generic Bleedout topic (039B95, code BLED) for bleedout lines. Around 190 vanilla generic lines (combat barks, crime and
  stealing comments) can fire for any custom NPC, so check which ones pass before adding your own.

## Testing without a keyboard

With `bDebug=1`, `sDebugAuto="N[t][a][:choice]"` runs a hands-free scenario once the world is populated: place the Nth faction's squad,
walk up to it, wait for the conversation, choose. `t` brings the leader to you, `a` arms you, `choice` is one of
pay, poor, refuse, fight, peace, defy, news, trade. The choice is made with synthetic keys in the game's own input queue, not the OS.
